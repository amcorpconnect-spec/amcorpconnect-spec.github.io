Organise

Support for Organise: File Manager on iPhone, iPhone Duo and iPad.

Developer: Akshay Meena · Feature availability depends on your installed version.

Where are my files?

Organise Files contains files saved in this app’s Documents folder. Device Library brings together the photos, videos and selected device folders you have authorised. Filters organise the view; they do not create extra copies. iOS does not let third-party file managers browse other apps’ private storage.

External drives and iCloud

Use Connect Drive and choose a drive or folder in Apple’s picker. The saved location then appears by its name. iCloud Drive and available file providers are also accessed through Apple’s picker. A disconnected drive or revoked permission may need reconnecting. The app never treats an unconnected drive as available.

USB transfer with a Mac

Connect and trust your iPhone or iPad, then open Finder → your device → Files → Organise. Drag files between the Mac and Organise’s shared Documents folder. First copy Photos originals or files from another location into Organise Files if you want to transfer them through Finder. Finder controls its own progress. No Organise Connect companion or computer-pairing key is required.

Interrupted transfers

In-app transfers preserve recovery information. Completed files are not needlessly copied again. Resuming within a file depends on support from both source and destination; otherwise that file may restart. Reconnect the same location before resuming. Finder and AirDrop transfers are controlled by Apple and do not use Organise’s recovery engine. Keep originals until the destination has been checked.

App lock and Vault

Your app PIN is required. Face ID or Touch ID can be enabled in app security settings when the device supports it and Apple permits access. Vault uses its own PIN and encrypted storage. Neither support nor a subscription can recover a forgotten Vault secret or restore files you have no backup of.

Free and Organise Pro

The free version includes advertising and requires internet. Monthly or yearly Pro removes ads and allows local offline access. Cloud and network storage still require a connection. Settings → Organise Pro shows your verified active plan, period end or renewal date, purchase restoration and Apple subscription management. A monthly subscriber can switch to yearly; Apple confirms when the change takes effect. Cancelling renewal does not remove access before the paid period ends.

Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that bought the subscription. Restoring purchases restores access, not deleted files or Vault data. If paid access expires or is revoked, the free version and its connectivity requirement apply again. Prices and renewal terms shown by Apple at checkout take precedence.

Report a problem

Email amcorpconnect@gmail.com with your app version, device, iOS version and the steps that caused the problem. A screenshot with private details hidden can help. Do not send PINs, passwords or private files.
