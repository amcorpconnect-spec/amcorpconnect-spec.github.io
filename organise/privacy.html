Privacy Policy

Organise: File Manager · Developer: Akshay Meena · Updated 14 November 2026

Organise helps you manage files, authorised photos and videos, connected folders, drives and network storage. This policy covers the Organise app and these support pages. Features and availability may vary by released version.

Your files and permissions

Files, thumbnails, file indexes, connected-folder bookmarks, favourites, settings and transfer recovery records are stored on your device. We do not operate an Organise account system or a server that receives your file library. Organise does not have unrestricted access to iOS internal storage or other apps’ private data.

Photos access follows the full or limited access you grant through Apple. Folder, drive and cloud access follows the folders you choose through Apple’s picker. You can change Photos permission in system Settings and disconnect saved locations in Organise. Revoking access may stop a pending transfer. Local-network permission is used for network connections you choose.

When you copy, move, export or share, content goes to the destination you choose, such as iCloud, a network server, an external drive or another app. Those services and recipients have their own policies. Organise does not send filenames, photo content or document content to Google as ad targeting information.

PIN, biometrics and Vault

The app PIN protects opening Organise. Optional Face ID or Touch ID uses Apple’s authentication system; Organise receives a success or failure result, not biometric templates. PIN verification material and connection secrets use the device Keychain. Vault has a separate PIN and encrypts its stored files and thumbnail data. The app-unlock PIN does not itself encrypt every ordinary file.

We cannot recover a forgotten PIN or decrypt your Vault for you. Keychain records may remain after uninstalling the app. Keep separate backups of important files and protect the devices and servers you connect.

Advertising in the free version

The free version requires an internet connection and uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Google’s SDK may process IP addresses, device or app-scoped identifiers, ad impressions and interactions, diagnostic information and performance data for advertising, fraud prevention and measurement. An IP address can indicate approximate location. The app disables the SDK’s optional coarse-location collection, publisher ad personalisation and publisher first-party ID. It does not request Apple’s tracking permission.

Where required, Google’s consent form offers regional privacy choices before ads can be requested. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, it appears in Organise Settings as “Ad Privacy Choices”. Refusing optional consent does not itself lock your files. Ad availability depends on consent, connectivity and Google’s response.

A verified active Pro subscription stops new ad requests and removes ad placements. A request already in progress or SDK processing does not become a promise that no technical data can ever be processed. If paid access expires or is revoked, the free ad-supported mode becomes eligible again.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s iOS SDK data disclosure.

Subscriptions and internet checks

Apple handles payments, subscription billing and purchase restoration. Organise checks Apple-verified transactions and subscription status to enable Pro. We do not receive your payment card details. Prices and renewal terms are shown in the App Store purchase flow. You can manage or cancel a subscription through your Apple Account.

Pro permits offline access to local files. Cloud and network operations still need their relevant connections. To check internet availability, Organise makes a small HTTPS request to an Apple-hosted connectivity page; this request does not contain your files.

Retention and deletion

Use Organise’s file, history, Recently Deleted and saved-location controls to manage local records. A pending recovery job may retain temporary data until completed or cancelled. Removing the app removes its app-container data according to iOS behaviour, but does not delete exported files, external-drive files, cloud copies, recipient copies or all Keychain records. Apple’s subscription records remain governed by Apple.

Support, hosting and your choices

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send and use it to respond. Do not send PINs, passwords, Vault secrets or private file contents. Depending on your region, you may have rights to access, correct or delete information you have sent us; contact us at the address below.

These pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. They do not add advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub may process technical hosting data such as IP addresses under its Privacy Statement. The app is a general-purpose file utility and is not designed specifically for children. We update this page when relevant practices change.
