Getting started

Choose your language, subscribe, then set up the app lock and a separate import access PIN. There is no app account or free trial. Create each vault with your own name and chosen lock layers. Password hints are optional for each vault.

How do the lock layers work?

Every selected layer is required. When Face ID or Touch ID is unavailable or biometric enrollment changes, use the recovery PIN for that layer and enter the other selected credentials. A hint cannot decrypt your vault. If a biometric key changes, recover access and update the affected protection before making a new backup.

Importing photos, videos and files

Open the destination vault and folder. Choose Import from the menu or Add, select files with the Apple picker, and enter the import access PIN. The selection is authorized once. Locking the app or changing the destination ends that authorization. Duplicate files offer Skip or Keep Both.

Moving originals safely

Classified Vaults removes an original only after its encrypted copy is saved and verified. Changed, inaccessible, unverified or skipped originals are kept. Photos may ask you to confirm deletion, and deleted originals may remain in the Photos app’s own Recently Deleted area. Review that area yourself if you wish to remove those copies.

Camera and scanned documents

Open a vault, then choose Camera to take a photo, record video with audio, or scan pages to PDF. Enable camera access in system Settings; video with sound also needs microphone access. Captures are saved directly into the vault, without being added to Photos.

Backup and a new device

Use Backup to create an encrypted archive with a separate strong backup password. Choose iCloud Drive or another Files location and wait for saving to finish. To restore, select that archive and enter its backup password, then set up app protection on the new device. Individual vault credentials remain required; keep their biometric recovery PINs too. Classified Vaults has no central recovery service.

Forgotten credentials

Try your optional vault hint. If biometrics cannot be used, use the recovery PIN with all other selected locks. Keep the backup password separately. The developer cannot decrypt files if all usable credentials or required backup passwords are lost. Do not uninstall while trying to recover local data.

Recently Deleted

Deleted items remain encrypted under their containing vault’s protection. Restore them from Recently Deleted or confirm permanent deletion. Items become eligible for automatic permanent cleanup after 30 days when the protected vault is opened. Existing exported backups can still contain old copies.

An import stopped or storage is full

Check the Storage section, unlock the destination and start a new import selection. The app preserves originals when import does not safely complete. Verified files already committed before an interruption may be present; duplicate detection lets you skip them when retrying.

Subscriptions and purchases

An active annual or monthly subscription is required. Prices come from your Apple storefront. Use Restore purchases for the same Apple Account and Manage subscription to cancel renewal. Expiry locks access without automatically erasing your library. Apple handles payments, refunds and billing support.

Contact support

Email amcorpconnect@gmail.com with the app version, device model, OS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Include a screenshot only if it contains no private information. Never send passwords, recovery PINs, backup passwords or private vault files.
