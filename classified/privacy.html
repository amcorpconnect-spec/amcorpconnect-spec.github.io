Your files and account

Classified Vaults is published by Akshay Meena. No app account is required. Your vault files, names, hints and keys stay in the app’s private storage. We operate no server that receives this library and include no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

Permissions and biometrics

You choose files through Apple’s system pickers. Camera and microphone access is requested for capture you start. Photos deletion permission is requested only when moving verified imports. Apple performs Face ID or Touch ID authentication; Classified Vaults receives no face or fingerprint data. You can change permissions in system Settings.

Encryption and temporary files

Committed files, names and thumbnails are encrypted. Previewing or importing can create temporary readable files in protected private storage. The app clears its preview and import staging files on lock, cancellation or startup. Files are not automatically added to Photos or exposed through the Files app.

Backups and sharing

Backups are encrypted with the backup password and leave the app only when you choose a destination or share them. iCloud Drive and other providers handle transfer and storage under their own policies. Exported readable copies are outside vault protection. A restored backup retains independent vault locks; the backup password cannot replace those credentials.

Retention and deletion

Deleted vault items remain encrypted in Recently Deleted. They become eligible for automatic permanent deletion after 30 days; cleanup occurs when the protected vault is opened. You can permanently delete them sooner. A previous encrypted library may be retained locally after restoration for recovery. Uninstalling removes local app data; exported backups, shared copies and some system Keychain items may remain. Manage those copies with their provider. We hold no central copy to recover or erase for you.

Subscriptions

Apple processes subscriptions, payments, refunds and purchase restoration. The app checks verified transaction information to determine access. We do not receive your payment-card number through the app. Apple’s privacy and purchase terms apply. Losing subscription access does not automatically erase your encrypted library.

Support and website

If you email support, we receive the address, message and attachments you send and use them to answer your request. Do not send passwords, recovery secrets or private vault files. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request and applicable obligations. You can contact us about access or deletion of support correspondence. Hosting and email providers process traffic under their own policies.

Policy updates

This policy describes the app’s current behavior and may change with future versions. The published policy will show its update date. For privacy questions, contact Akshay Meena at amcorpconnect@gmail.com.
